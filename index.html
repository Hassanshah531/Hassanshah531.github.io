02RESEARCH PERSPECTIVE

Connecting expertise.
Creating shared value.

Different disciplines. Shared questions. A clear human purpose.

I study how AI can make complex evidence more useful, whether that evidence comes from medical images, infrastructure sensors or generated media. These settings demand careful interpretation, reliable methods and an understanding of the people who use the results.

My approach is to start with a practical question, work across disciplines and communicate both the findings and their limits.

Knowledge sharingOrganizing, presenting and practical teaching Peer-reviewed researchExplore publications and collaborative work Book contributionSmart Infrastructure Management · Elsevier

HOW I FRAME RESEARCH

From difficult data to useful evidence.

Start with field conditions
Low light, limited labels and noisy measurements shape the research question.
Connect complementary evidence
Bring images, 3D geometry and physical knowledge into the same assessment.
Make the limits visible
Explain what a method supports, where uncertainty remains and what still needs testing.
